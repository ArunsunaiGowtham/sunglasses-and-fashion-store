Privacy Policy

How Solaris & Co. safeguards client confidentiality, personal data, and transaction security.

Our Privacy Commitment

Effective Date: October 2026 • Validated for Solaris & Co. Global Clients

At Solaris & Co., your privacy is of paramount importance. We are committed to transparency, strict data confidentiality, and bank-grade security standards. We do not sell, rent, or monetize client personal data under any circumstances.

1. Information We Collect
  • Contact Details: Name, shipping address, email, and phone number for order dispatch and courier tracking.
  • Sizing Preferences: Eyewear frame measurements, belt length choices, and saved wishlist items.
  • Payment Information: Processed through PCI-DSS Level-1 certified encrypted gateways (we never store raw card numbers).
2. How We Use Information
  • Order Fulfillment: Processing transactions, preparing hand-packaged shipments, and sending tracking updates.
  • Client Concierge: Answering styling questions, fitting adjustments, and order inquiries.
  • Curated Updates: Opt-in private preview invitations and seasonal lookbooks (unsubscribable anytime).
3. Security & Protection
  • 256-Bit SSL Encryption: Every browser session, checkout step, and account login is fully encrypted.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing brokers.
  • Restricted Access: Client profiles are accessible strictly by authorized atelier fulfillment specialists.
4. Your Rights & Controls
  • Access & Portability: Request a digital copy of all personal information on file at any time.
  • Right to Deletion: Request complete deletion of your account and transaction history.
  • Privacy Contact: For inquiries, contact our Privacy Concierge at concierge@solaris-accessories.com.
