Our Heritage & Soul

Artisanship Without Compromise

Since 2014

Crafting the Accessories That Define Your Silhouette

Solaris & Co. was founded on a simple conviction: accessories should never be superficial afterthoughts. They are the defining punctuation marks of personal style.

From our founding atelier overlooking the Arno River in Florence to our design studio on Fifth Avenue in New York, we marry old-world European hand-craftsmanship with modern optical engineering. Every pair of sunglasses is fitted with laboratory-tested polarized mineral lenses; every leather belt is hand-burnished from single-cut Tuscan hides that age into heirloom patinas.

Our Mission

To free modern dressers from disposable fast-fashion accessories by producing architectural eyewear, genuine full-grain belts, and tailored headwear designed to endure decades of daily rotation.

Our Vision

To be the benchmark global brand where discerning men and women turn whenever they seek certified optical UV400 clarity, hand-stitched leathercraft, and timeless streetwear elegance.

Fashion Philosophy

We champion quiet luxury and effortless sprezzatura: subtle distinction achieved through organic texture, precise proportion, and authentic materials rather than loud, garish branding.
