Preserving Luxury Craftsmanship

Solaris Atelier Standard • Built for Decades of Distinction

Every piece in our collection—from hand-carved Mazzucchelli acetate to vegetable-tanned bovine leather—is engineered for longevity. Following these simple care protocols ensures your accessories retain their original luster and durability.

1. Eyewear & Lens Care
  • Microfiber Cleaning: Always rinse lenses with lukewarm water before wiping with the provided microfiber cloth to prevent micro-scratches.
  • Heat Protection: Avoid storing sunglasses on vehicle dashboards where summer cabin temperatures degrade polarized adhesives.
  • Hard Case Storage: Store in the lined magnetic clamshell case when not in use to preserve temple alignment and hinge tension.
2. Artisan Leather Belts
  • Moisture Management: Keep full-grain bovine leather away from direct water; if wet, blot dry with a soft cloth and dry at room temperature.
  • Periodic Conditioning: Apply a natural beeswax balm or leather conditioner once every 6 months to maintain supple elasticity.
  • Rest & Storage: Hang belts vertically by the buckle or coil loosely—never fold sharply or crease across notches.
3. 12-Month Atelier Warranty
  • Coverage: 1-year complimentary warranty against material defects, hinge malfunctions, loose screws, and buckle plating issues.
  • Free Tightening: Visit our Fifth Avenue flagship for lifetime ultrasonic cleaning and hinge tightening.
  • Exclusions: Accidental crushing, lens scratches from abrasive fabrics, and intentional modifications.
4. Returns & Exchanges
  • 30-Day Window: Items in unworn condition with original tags and protective packaging are eligible for return or exchange within 30 days.
  • Prepaid Return Shipping: Complimentary insured return labels provided for domestic size and frame swaps.
  • Prompt Refunds: Approved returns processed to your original payment method within 3 business days of atelier receipt.
